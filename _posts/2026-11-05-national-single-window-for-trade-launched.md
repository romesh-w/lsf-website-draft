Sri Lanka's National Single Window for Trade was launched on 5 November 2026 under the leadership of the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development. The system is built on [OpenNSW]({{ '/projects/OpenNSW/' | relative_url }}), the Lanka Software Foundation's open source framework for building national single window systems.
